The change is made in the `PALETTES.dark` object (roles such as `accent`, `peri`, `hot`, `glint`,
`deep`), not by editing colors inside individual figures. The accent becomes the teal. The
support accent `peri` is a neighbor of teal on the color wheel (for example cyan or a blue-green),
not a complementary color such as orange or red. `hot` becomes a very light, mostly white teal.
